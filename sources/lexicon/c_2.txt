   a tennis club - теннисный клуб
2. ночной клуб - a place to dance at night
   They went to a club.
3. дубинка - a heavy stick used as a weapon
   He was hit with a club.
4. трефы (в картах) - one of the four suits in cards
   the ace of clubs
